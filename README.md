One sentence explaining the modern internet to a traveler who has just stepped out of the year 1800 — written for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) one-liner competition, using only words and pictures a person from 1800 could already hold in their head: a great city square, its shops and newspapers, and a chair at home.

## How to run

```
node main.ts
```

The program writes the entry line to `/output/entry.txt` (no trailing newline) and prints it to stdout. No dependencies — Node 22 only.

## The entry line

> Think of a great city square where every shop, newspaper, and friend you have ever known stands ready to talk, and you can reach any of them from your own chair.
